About Prime Clean

Prime Clean is a remote-managed cleaning service. We connect people who need cleaning with vetted, professional cleaners — and we manage everything in between. Clients get a single, reliable point of contact; cleaners get steady work without the stress of finding clients. We handle the contracts, the matching, the scheduling and the quality, so every job goes smoothly for both sides.
