# Prime Clean

Website for Prime Clean — a remote-managed cleaning service that connects
clients with vetted cleaners and manages every job in between.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `how-it-works.html` — How the service works (clients, cleaners, the model)
- `book.html` — Book a cleaning (client request form)
- `cleaners.html` — Become a cleaner (application form)
- `contact.html` — Contact & about

## Before going live
Search the files for `TODO` and replace the placeholders:
- `hello@primeclean.example` → your real email address
- `15550000000` → your real WhatsApp number (digits only, with country code, no `+`)

The booking and application forms have no backend — they assemble the
visitor's details into a pre-filled WhatsApp or email message to send to you.
