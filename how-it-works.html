How Prime Clean Works

Prime Clean is the middle layer between people who need cleaning and the professionals who do it. We find the work, vet the people, and manage every job — remotely.

For clients

Getting your space cleaned takes three simple steps.

1

Request a cleaning

Tell us the space, the date and what you need. It takes about a minute.

2

We match & confirm

We assign a vetted cleaner, agree the price with you, and lock in the time.

3

Sit back

The cleaner does the work. We follow up to make sure you're happy.

For cleaners

We bring you steady work — you focus on cleaning.

1

Join the network

Apply once, get verified, and tell us your area and availability.

2

Get matched to jobs

We secure the contracts and send the right jobs your way.

3

Clean & earn

Do great work and receive your share for every completed job.

The Prime Clean model

A simple, transparent split. Prime Clean secures and manages the contract; the cleaner does the work. Both sides earn a fair share of every job.

🤝

Client pays Prime Clean

One agreed price for a fully managed, hassle-free service.

⚙️

Prime Clean manages it

We source the contract, match the cleaner, handle scheduling and quality, and keep our service share.

🧹

Cleaner gets paid

The cleaner receives their share for completing the job to standard.
